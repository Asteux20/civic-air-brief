    How to read your brief
    Band
    India's CPCB scale: Good, Satisfactory, Moderate, Poor, Very Poor, Severe. Poor and worse affects most people on long exposure.
    2.9× limit
    The site average divided by the India 24-hour limit (PM2.5 60, PM10 100, NO2 80). Above 1× breaks the standard.
    Confidence
    High means many clean readings. Low means few readings or many flagged ones, so treat the site with care.
    F3 chips
    Each chip is a fact computed in code from your rows. Select it to see the exact rows.
    Uncertain
    The AI wrote a claim it could not tie to a fact. Read it as a lead, not a finding.
